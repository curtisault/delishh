## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish — a foil pan if it is going straight to the freezer
- Large pot, for the pasta
- Wide skillet or Dutch oven, for the sauce

## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 450 g bulk Italian sausage
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 800 g can crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tsp kosher salt

### Ricotta layer

- 425 g whole-milk ricotta — whole-milk only; see Watchpoints
- 1 large egg
- ½ cup grated parmesan
- ¼ cup fresh basil, chopped

### Pasta and cheese

- 450 g ziti
- 450 g whole-milk low-moisture mozzarella, shredded, divided
- ½ cup grated parmesan

## Steps

1. Boil the ziti in well-salted water for 3 minutes less than the
   package says, then drain. It finishes in the oven, and pasta
   cooked to al dente now is mush after the freezer.
   cue: 3 MIN SHORT OF THE PACKAGE · FIRM CENTER

2. Heat the oil over medium-high and brown the sausage, breaking it
   up, until it has real colour. Add the onion and cook until soft.
   cue: 10 MIN · BROWNED, ONION SOFT

3. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste and cook until the paste
   darkens a shade.
   cue: 1–2 MIN · PASTE DARKENED

4. Add the tomatoes, oregano, pepper flakes and salt. Simmer
   uncovered, stirring now and then, until it thickens a little.
   It should stay looser than a pasta sauce you would serve —
   the ziti drinks it in the oven.
   cue: 15 MIN · THICKENED, STILL LOOSE

5. Stir together the ricotta, egg, ½ cup parmesan and basil.
   cue: SMOOTH, NO DRY POCKETS

6. Fold the drained ziti through all but 1 cup of the sauce.
   Spread half in the pan. Dot the ricotta mixture over it in
   spoonfuls, then scatter half the mozzarella. Add the rest of the
   pasta, the reserved sauce, the rest of the mozzarella and the
   last ½ cup parmesan.
   cue: PASTA · RICOTTA · CHEESE · PASTA · SAUCE · CHEESE

7. To freeze: cool completely — counter, then uncovered in the
   fridge — then wrap. To bake now: cover with foil and bake at
   190 °C / 375 °F, then uncover until bubbling at the edges and
   spotted brown on top.
   cue: 30 MIN COVERED + 15 · BUBBLING, BROWN SPOTS

8. Rest before cutting so the layers hold.
   cue: 10 MIN · LAYERS HOLD

## Watchpoints

- Whole-milk ricotta only; part-skim turns grainy and weeps after freezing.
- Undercook the pasta by 3 minutes, every time — it is the difference between baked ziti and a pan of paste.
- Keep a cup of sauce back for the top layer, or the exposed pasta dries out and turns leathery.

## Rescues

- **Dry, stiff pasta on top** — No sauce on the top layer, or it baked uncovered too long. Spoon over warm sauce or a splash of water, cover, and give it 10 more minutes.
- **Mushy, falling apart** — The pasta was cooked through before baking. It cannot be undone; next time, 3 minutes short.
- **Watery pool under the slices** — Cut too soon, or part-skim ricotta. Rest a full 10 minutes.

## Keeps

- Freezer, unbaked: 3 months. Plastic pressed onto the surface, foil over the top; label with the bake times before it goes in.
- From frozen: 190 °C / 375 °F, 75 minutes covered, then 15 uncovered, until hot in the center.
- From thawed overnight in the fridge: 30 minutes covered, then 15 uncovered.
- Baked leftovers: fridge 4 days.

## Note

This is the one I make two of at a time — one for tonight, one for
the freezer, because the sauce takes the same effort either way. Hot
Italian sausage is my default; mild is fine if you're feeding kids,
and the pepper flakes can come out entirely.
