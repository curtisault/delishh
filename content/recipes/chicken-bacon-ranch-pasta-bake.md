## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Large pot, for the pasta
- Wide skillet or Dutch oven, for the bacon, chicken and sauce

## Ingredients

### Pasta and filling

- 450 g rotini
- 225 g bacon, chopped
- 900 g boneless chicken thighs, in 2 cm pieces

### Ranch blend

- 2 tsp dried dill
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- 1½ tsp garlic powder
- 1½ tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

### Sauce

- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups whole milk
- 115 g cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup sour cream
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

### Top

- 2 cups cheddar and Monterey Jack, shredded
- 3 scallions, sliced

## Steps

1. Boil the rotini in well-salted water for 3 minutes less than
   the package says, then drain.
   cue: 3 MIN SHORT OF THE PACKAGE · FIRM CENTER

2. Stir the ranch blend together in a small bowl.
   cue: ONE BOWL · EVENLY MIXED

3. In the skillet over medium, cook the bacon until crisp, then
   lift it out and leave the fat. Raise the heat, season the
   chicken with half the ranch blend, and brown it in the bacon
   fat in two batches until cooked through.
   cue: 15 MIN · BACON CRISP, CHICKEN BROWNED

4. Lower to medium. Melt the butter in the same pan, add the
   garlic for 30 seconds, then the flour, stirring until it smells
   toasty. Whisk in the broth slowly, then the milk, and simmer
   until it thickens.
   cue: 8 MIN · NO LUMPS, COATS A SPOON

5. Off the heat, whisk in the cream cheese, sour cream, lemon juice
   and the rest of the ranch blend until smooth. The sauce should
   look too loose — the pasta absorbs a lot of it in the freezer
   and the oven.
   cue: SMOOTH · LOOSER THAN YOU WANT

6. Fold the pasta, chicken and most of the bacon through the
   sauce. Spread in the pan and top with the cheese and the last of
   the bacon.
   cue: EVENLY COATED · CHEESE TO THE EDGES

7. To freeze: cool completely, then wrap. To bake now: 190 °C /
   375 °F, covered 25 minutes, then uncovered until bubbling and
   the cheese is browned in spots. Scatter the scallions to serve.
   cue: 25 MIN COVERED + 10 · BUBBLING, BROWN SPOTS

## Watchpoints

- Undercook the pasta by 3 minutes; cream sauces punish soft pasta harder than red ones.
- The cream cheese and sour cream go in off the heat — boiled, they split and turn grainy.
- Leave the sauce loose. A sauce that looks right in the pot bakes up dry.

## Rescues

- **Dry and tight after baking** — The pasta drank the sauce. Fold in a splash of warm milk and give it 5 more minutes covered.
- **Sauce grainy or split** — The dairy boiled. Whisk in a splash of cold milk off the heat; it improves, but it will not fully come back.
- **Bacon on top went soft in the freezer** — It does. Hold back a handful of cooked bacon in its own bag and add it for the uncovered stretch.

## Keeps

- Freezer, unbaked: 2 months — cream sauces start to weep past that. Plastic pressed onto the surface, foil over the top; label before it goes in.
- From frozen: 190 °C / 375 °F, 60 minutes covered, then 15 uncovered.
- From thawed overnight in the fridge: 25 minutes covered, then 10 uncovered.
- Baked leftovers: fridge 3 days.

## Note

The homemade ranch blend is better than the packet and you control
the salt. If you'd rather use a packet, it's about 3 tablespoons, and
cut the kosher salt to nothing. Penne works fine; rotini just holds
more sauce in the twists.
