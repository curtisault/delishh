## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Wide skillet or Dutch oven, for the chili
- Two bowls at bake time, for the cornbread — the batter is never made ahead

## Ingredients

### Chili — the only part that freezes

- 900 g ground beef, 85/15
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 425 g can crushed tomatoes
- 2 cans pinto or kidney beans, 425 g each, rinsed
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1½ tsp salt

### Cornbread lid — mixed at bake time, never frozen

- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 2 large eggs
- 4 tbsp melted butter
- 1 cup corn kernels
- 1 cup sharp cheddar, shredded

## Steps

1. Brown the beef in the oil over high heat, half at a time.
   Crowding the pan steams the meat and loses the crust entirely.
   cue: 10 MIN · IN BATCHES, REAL CRUST

2. Add the onion and pepper and cook 6 minutes, then the garlic and
   dry spices for 1 minute.
   cue: 7 MIN · SOFT, THEN SPICES BLOOMED

3. Stir in the tomato paste and let it darken before anything wet
   goes in.
   cue: 2 MIN · PASTE DARKENED

4. Add the crushed tomatoes, beans, broth, and salt. Simmer
   uncovered until scoopable, not soupy — it loosens again in the
   oven, and soupy chili floods the cornbread from below.
   cue: 20 MIN · SCOOPABLE, NOT SOUPY

5. Spread the chili in the pan, cool completely, and wrap. This is
   the whole freezer stage — no topping.
   cue: FREEZE BARE · THE LID COMES LATER

6. On baking day, whisk the dry ingredients; separately whisk the
   buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter. Combine in a few strokes,
   then fold in the corn and cheddar. A few strokes only —
   overmixed cornbread bakes tough and domed.
   cue: 5 MIN · A FEW STROKES, STILL LUMPY

7. Bake in two stages at 190 °C / 375 °F: heat the chili covered
   until bubbling, spread the batter over the hot chili, and return
   uncovered until a toothpick comes out clean. Cold chili under
   raw batter leaves the underside of the cornbread gluey — it must
   go on hot.
   cue: 60 MIN COVERED, THEN 30 · TOOTHPICK CLEAN

## Watchpoints

- The batter is always fresh. Cornbread batter made ahead and frozen bakes up dense and gray, and mixing it takes five minutes.
- The chili doubles cleanly in a wide pot; the cornbread does not. Mix a second batch rather than scaling one.
- Stage one is not optional — the batter only ever meets bubbling chili.

## Rescues

- **Cornbread gluey underneath** — The chili was not hot enough when the batter went on. Nothing fixes this pan; next time, bubbling means bubbling.
- **Cornbread tough and domed** — Overmixed. Stop while the batter is still lumpy.
- **Chili flooded the lid** — It went into the freezer soupy. Simmer to scoopable before the pan, every time.

## Keeps

- Freezer, chili only: 4 months. Cool completely — counter, then uncovered in the fridge — then plastic pressed flat, foil over the top. Label before it goes in.
- From frozen: 190 °C / 375 °F, 60 minutes covered, then the fresh batter and 30 uncovered.
- No buttermilk: 1 cup whole milk plus 1 tbsp vinegar, left 10 minutes. Real buttermilk is better, but this is close.

## Note

Serve with sour cream, pickled jalapeños, scallions, and hot honey
over the cornbread. For a meatless pan, swap the beef for a pound of
mushrooms browned hard plus a third can of beans — and keep the
smoked paprika; it carries the whole thing.
