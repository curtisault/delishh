## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Wide skillet, for the sausage
- Clean kitchen towel, for squeezing the hash browns
- Instant-read thermometer — done is 74 °C / 165 °F in the center, checked

## Ingredients

### Base

- 450 g bulk breakfast sausage
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 900 g frozen shredded hash browns, thawed
- 2 cups sharp cheddar, shredded, divided
- 3 scallions, sliced

### Custard

- 10 large eggs
- 2 cups whole milk
- ½ tsp dry mustard
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

## Steps

1. Brown the sausage, breaking it small. Add the onion and pepper
   and cook until soft, then drain the fat and let it cool — warm
   sausage starts cooking the eggs.
   cue: 12 MIN · BROWNED, THEN COOLED

2. Wring the thawed hash browns out in the towel, in batches, until
   no more liquid runs. This is the step that decides whether the
   pan sets or weeps.
   cue: WRUNG UNTIL NOTHING RUNS

3. Spread the hash browns in the pan, then the sausage mixture,
   half the cheddar and the scallions.
   cue: POTATO · SAUSAGE · CHEESE

4. Whisk the eggs, milk, mustard, paprika, salt and pepper until
   no streaks of white are left, and pour evenly over. Scatter the
   rest of the cheddar.
   cue: UNIFORM, NO STREAKS OF WHITE

5. To freeze: wrap now, unbaked. To bake now: 175 °C / 350 °F,
   covered 35 minutes, then uncovered until set in the center and
   golden on top.
   cue: 35 MIN COVERED + 20 · 165 °F CENTER

6. Rest before cutting so the custard sets.
   cue: 10 MIN · SET, NOT SLUMPING

## Watchpoints

- Wring the hash browns dry; wet potatoes are the whole reason egg casseroles weep.
- Cool the sausage before the custard goes over it.
- Two months is the freezer wall for egg custard — past it, the eggs turn watery and spongy.

## Rescues

- **Watery and weeping** — The hash browns went in wet, or it froze past two months. Wring them dry next time; drain the pan juices and serve.
- **Raw in the middle at the time given** — Pans and ovens vary. Back in covered, 10 minutes at a time, until the center reads 74 °C / 165 °F.
- **Rubbery eggs** — Baked past done. Pull it at temperature, not at the clock.

## Keeps

- Freezer, unbaked: 2 months. Plastic pressed onto the surface, foil over the top; label before it goes in.
- From frozen: 175 °C / 350 °F, 75 minutes covered, then 25 uncovered, to 74 °C / 165 °F in the center.
- From thawed overnight in the fridge: 35 minutes covered, then 20 uncovered.
- Baked leftovers: fridge 4 days; squares reheat well in the microwave.

## Note

The strata's weekday cousin — no soak, no stale bread, and it goes
from the freezer to the oven on a Sunday morning without you thinking
about it the night before. Hot sauce at the table is not optional in
my house.
