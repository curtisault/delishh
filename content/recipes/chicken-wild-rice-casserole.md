## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Wide pot or Dutch oven, for the sauce
- Saucepan, for the rice

## Ingredients

### Casserole

- 1 cup wild rice blend
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 ribs celery, diced
- 225 g cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1½ cups whole milk
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 4 cups cooked chicken, chopped
- 1½ cups Gruyère or Swiss, shredded from the block — never pre-shredded
- 1½ tsp salt

### Topping — added at bake time, never frozen

- 1 cup panko
- 2 tbsp melted butter
- ¼ cup grated parmesan

## Steps

1. Cook the rice blend to a shade under package doneness — still a
   bit of bite — and drain well. Wild rice keeps softening in
   sauce, in the freezer and again in the oven.
   cue: A BIT OF BITE LEFT, DRAINED WELL

2. Butter over medium-high. Leave the mushrooms undisturbed until
   browned, then add the onion, carrot, and celery for 8 minutes,
   and the garlic and thyme for 1.
   cue: 10 MIN · MUSHROOMS BROWNED FIRST

3. Stir in the flour and cook it out.
   cue: 2 MIN · NO RAW FLOUR SMELL

4. Add the broth gradually, whisking, then the milk. Simmer until
   it thickly coats a spoon.
   cue: 6 MIN · THICKLY COATS A SPOON

5. Off the heat, stir in the cheese until smooth, then fold in the
   rice, chicken, and salt.
   cue: OFF THE HEAT · SMOOTH, THEN FOLD

6. Spread in the pan, cool completely, and wrap — no topping yet.
   Frozen panko drinks moisture from the sauce and bakes into a
   soft gray layer instead of a crisp one.
   cue: FREEZE BARE · CRUMBS COME LATER

7. At bake time, stir the panko with the melted butter and
   parmesan, scatter it over the hot casserole, and bake uncovered
   until deep gold.
   cue: 20 MIN · DEEP GOLD CRUST

## Watchpoints

- The panko takes thirty seconds to mix on the day, and it is the difference between a crust and a paste.
- Block cheese only. The anti-caking starch on pre-shredded goes grainy on reheat.
- Pull the rice early, every time — it absorbs liquid right up until it is served.

## Rescues

- **Rice gone to mush** — It was cooked to done before assembly. There is no fix in this pan; underboil the next one.
- **Sauce grainy after reheat** — Pre-shredded cheese, or the cheese went into a boiling pot. Block cheese, off the heat.
- **Topping soft and gray** — The crumbs rode through the freezer. They only ever go on at bake time.

## Keeps

- Freezer, no topping: 3 months. Cool completely — counter, then uncovered in the fridge — then plastic pressed flat, foil over the top. Label before it goes in.
- From frozen: 190 °C / 375 °F, 70 minutes covered, then the crumbs and 20 uncovered. From thawed: 25 plus 15.
- The rice can be cooked and refrigerated 3 days ahead, which turns this into a 30-minute assembly.

## Note

Serve with roasted broccoli or green beans with lemon. Gruyère is
worth it, but sharp white cheddar works. No mushrooms in the house:
leave them out, add a second carrot, and brown a tablespoon of
tomato paste with the roux to keep the savory depth.
