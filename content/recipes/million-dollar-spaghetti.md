## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Large pot, for the spaghetti
- Wide skillet, for the meat sauce
- Mixing bowl, for the cheese layer

## Ingredients

### Meat sauce

- 454 g ground beef
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp Italian seasoning
- 1360 g marinara sauce, two 680 g jars

### Cheese layer

- 225 g cream cheese, softened
- 225 g cottage cheese
- 225 g sour cream, full-fat

### Assembly

- 454 g spaghetti
- 8 tbsp butter, cut into 8 pats
- ½ cup sharp cheddar, shredded

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C / 350 °F. Boil the spaghetti to the time
   on the package, then drain it well.
   cue: PACKAGE TIME · DRAINED WELL

2. Brown the beef in the skillet over medium-high with the salt,
   garlic powder and Italian seasoning, breaking it up as it cooks,
   until no pink is left.
   cue: BROWNED, NO PINK LEFT

3. Pour off the fat, then stir in the marinara and bring it up to
   hot.
   cue: FAT POURED OFF · SAUCE HOT THROUGH

4. Beat the softened cream cheese in the bowl until smooth, then mix
   in the cottage cheese and sour cream.
   cue: SMOOTH, NO STREAKS OF CREAM CHEESE

5. Lay the butter pats over the bottom of the dish. Add half the
   spaghetti, spread the cheese mixture over it, cover with the rest
   of the spaghetti, and spoon all of the meat sauce over the top. To
   freeze, wrap it here and keep the cheddar back.
   cue: BUTTER · PASTA · CHEESE · PASTA · SAUCE

6. Bake uncovered.
   cue: 30 MIN · UNCOVERED, HOT THROUGH

7. Scatter the cheddar over the top and bake until it has melted.
   cue: 15 MIN · CHEDDAR MELTED

## Watchpoints

- The cream cheese is softened before it is mixed; cold, it stays in lumps through the whole layer.
- Pour the beef fat off before the sauce goes in, or it pools in the bottom of the dish on top of the butter.
- The cheddar goes on at the 30-minute mark, not at the start, so it melts without browning hard.

## Rescues

- **Lumpy cheese layer** — The cream cheese was cold. Beat it on its own until smooth, then add the cottage cheese and sour cream.
- **Greasy pool in the corners** — Beef fat left in the sauce, on top of the butter. Spoon it off now, and drain the beef next time.

## Keeps

- Freezer, unbaked: 1 month. Assemble through the meat sauce, keep the cheddar back, and wrap the dish.
- To bake: thaw it in the fridge through the day, then bake as written — 30 minutes uncovered, the cheddar on, 15 more.

## Note

Not a million dollars of anything — it is buttered spaghetti with a
cheese layer through the middle, and that is exactly why the pan
comes back empty. The butter on the bottom is the part nobody
believes until they get a corner piece. On a week that is going to
be busy, I build it the night before, freeze it, and move it to the
fridge in the morning so it is ready to bake by dinner.
