## Equipment

- Dutch oven or heavy pot
- Quart freezer bags — flat bags, not a dish
- A sink that fits the pot, for the ice-water chill

## Ingredients

### Chili

- 900 g beef chuck, cut in 1.5 cm cubes
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 yellow onions, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 chipotles in adobo, minced
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 3 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 800 g can crushed tomatoes
- 3 cans black beans, 425 g each, rinsed
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 2 tsp salt

### To finish

- 1 tbsp masa harina or fine cornmeal
- cilantro, red onion, cotija, lime

## Steps

1. Pat the beef dry, salt it, and brown hard in three batches.
   Dry meat and an uncrowded pan are the whole difference between
   browning and steaming.
   cue: 15 MIN · THREE BATCHES, REAL CRUST

2. Set the beef aside. Cook the onions in the fat until golden,
   scraping the bottom.
   cue: 10 MIN · GOLDEN, FOND SCRAPED UP

3. Add the garlic, chili powder, and cumin for a minute, then the
   tomato paste and chipotles for two.
   cue: 3 MIN · PASTE DARKENED, CHILES IN

4. Return the beef with its juices. Add the crushed tomatoes,
   broth, brown sugar, and salt, and simmer partly covered until
   the beef shreds under a spoon.
   cue: 90 MIN · SHREDS UNDER A SPOON

5. Stir in the black beans and simmer. Beans added at the start
   disintegrate over ninety minutes — late is right.
   cue: 20 MIN · BEANS LATE, STILL WHOLE

6. Stir the masa with 2 tbsp water, stir it in, and simmer to
   lose the raw taste.
   cue: 5 MIN · THICKENED, NO RAW CORN TASTE

7. Cool the pot in a sink of ice water, uncovered, before
   packing. A warm pot in the freezer forms large ice crystals
   that water the chili down on reheat.
   cue: ICE BATH · COLD BEFORE IT PACKS

## Watchpoints

- Cubed chuck, not ground, is the card. Ground chuck cuts the simmer to 45 minutes — less texture, still very good on a Tuesday.
- The garnish is structural: cotija, thin-sliced red onion, cilantro, lime. Dense, smoky chili needs the acid and the raw bite.
- No masa in the house: fine cornmeal, or crush two corn tortillas into the pot — both thicken and add the right toasted-corn note.

## Rescues

- **Beef still tough at ninety minutes** — It needs more time, not more heat. Keep the bare simmer going; it gives up eventually.
- **Beans dissolved** — They went in at the start. Last twenty minutes only.
- **Watery on reheat** — The pot was packed warm. Simmer it back down uncovered, and ice-bath the next batch.

## Keeps

- Freezer, in bags: 4 months. Ladle 4 cups into a quart bag, press the air out, lay flat until solid, then file upright like books. Label before it goes in.
- From frozen: straight to the stove, covered, 25 minutes. From thawed: 10–12.
- Genuinely better on month two — not a figure of speech. The chipotle heat settles and spreads out over a few weeks instead of hitting in one spot.

## Note

Serve with cotija, red onion, cilantro, and lime — or over rice, or
under a fried egg. This is the card to double when the freezer is
being stocked for real: it is the one that improves in there.
