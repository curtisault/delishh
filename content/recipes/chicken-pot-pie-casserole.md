## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Wide pot or Dutch oven, for the filling
- Pastry brush, for the egg wash

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 6 tbsp butter
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 carrots, sliced thin
- 2 ribs celery, diced
- 225 g mushrooms, quartered
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 4 cups cooked chicken, chopped
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

### Lid — frozen separately, always

- 2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
- 1 large egg, beaten with 1 tbsp water
- flaky salt

## Steps

1. Melt the butter over medium. Cook the onion, carrots, celery,
   and mushrooms until the mushroom liquid has cooked off and the
   pan is dry again. Wet vegetables thin the sauce, and a thin
   sauce under pastry is a soggy lid.
   cue: 12 MIN · PAN DRY AGAIN

2. Sprinkle in the flour and cook, stirring.
   cue: 2 MIN · NO RAW FLOUR SMELL

3. Add the broth slowly, whisking out lumps, then the cream and
   thyme. Simmer until thick.
   cue: 8 MIN · THICK, HOLDS A TRAIL

4. Off the heat, fold in the chicken, peas, salt, and pepper.
   Taste now — it should read slightly over-seasoned. Cold dulls
   salt, and what tastes right warm tastes flat straight out of
   the freezer.
   cue: OFF THE HEAT · SLIGHTLY OVER-SEASONED

5. Spread in the pan and cool completely. Freeze the filling here;
   the pastry stays in its own box, unrolled and flat. Pastry
   frozen on wet filling steams instead of puffing and bakes out
   pale.
   cue: FILLING AND PASTRY NEVER FREEZE TOGETHER

6. Heat the filling covered at 205 °C / 400 °F until bubbling. Lay
   the pastry over, brush with the egg wash, scatter flaky salt,
   and cut four real vents — slashes an inch long, through to the
   filling. Sealed pastry traps steam and the underside turns to
   paste; the vents are not decorative.
   cue: FILLING BUBBLING BEFORE THE LID GOES ON

7. Return uncovered until deep golden and puffed, and rest 10
   minutes before cutting.
   cue: 30 MIN · DEEP GOLDEN, THEN REST 10

## Watchpoints

- Filling and pastry freeze separately, always. The filling in the dish, cooled and double-wrapped; the pastry flat in its box so it does not crack.
- The pastry only ever meets hot filling — the same rule as every two-stage bake in this set.
- Cool completely before wrapping: counter, then uncovered in the fridge until cold.

## Rescues

- **Pale, flat pastry** — It went onto cool filling, or the vents never got cut. Heat stage one to actually bubbling and slash through to the filling.
- **Underside of the lid is paste** — Steam had nowhere to go. Four vents, an inch each, all the way through.
- **Filling thin and watery** — The vegetables went in wet, or the simmer got cut short. Simmer the sauce until it holds a trail before anything folds in.

## Keeps

- Freezer, filling only: 3 months. Plastic pressed flat to the surface, foil over the top; label before it goes in.
- From frozen: 205 °C / 400 °F, 65 minutes covered, then pastry and 30 uncovered. Rest 10.
- Freeze flat and stack once solid.

## Note

It is a full plate on its own — a bitter green salad if you want
one. Drop-biscuit dough works in place of the pastry and is more
forgiving, under the same rule: onto hot filling, never frozen in
place. Leftover roast turkey is a straight substitution and better
for it; add a bay leaf to the sauce.
