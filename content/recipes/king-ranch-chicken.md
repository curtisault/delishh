## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Wide saucepan or Dutch oven, for the sauce

## Ingredients

### Sauce — a butter roux and cream cheese, not condensed soup

- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 2 ribs celery, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup whole milk
- 115 g cream cheese, cubed
- 285 g can Ro-Tel, drained well — hard; see Watchpoints

### Spice and body

- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1½ tsp cumin
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 cups shredded rotisserie chicken
- 18 corn tortillas, torn in quarters
- 3 cups cheddar and Monterey Jack, shredded

## Steps

1. Melt the butter over medium. Cook the onion, pepper, and celery
   until soft and starting to color, then add the garlic and dry
   spices and stir 1 minute.
   cue: 8 MIN · SOFT, EDGES COLORING

2. Sprinkle the flour over and cook, stirring constantly, until it
   smells like pie crust.
   cue: 2 MIN · SMELLS LIKE PIE CRUST

3. Add the broth in a slow stream, whisking, then the milk. Simmer
   until it coats a spoon.
   cue: 5 MIN · COATS A SPOON

4. Off the heat, stir in the cream cheese until smooth, then the
   drained Ro-Tel and salt. Off the heat matters — boiled cream
   cheese breaks into oil and grit, and freezing makes it worse.
   cue: OFF THE HEAT · SMOOTH, NO LUMPS

5. Fold the shredded chicken through the sauce.
   cue: EVERY SHRED COATED

6. Spread a thin scrape of sauce in the pan, then a third of the
   tortillas, a third of the chicken mixture, a third of the cheese.
   Repeat twice.
   cue: TORTILLAS · CHICKEN · CHEESE, THREE TIMES

7. Press the top gently so everything is in contact, then cool and
   wrap for the freezer — air pockets inside the casserole are where
   freezer burn starts. Or bake now at 175 °C / 350 °F, uncovered,
   until bubbling.
   cue: 35 MIN · PRESSED FLAT, THEN BUBBLING

## Watchpoints

- The roux-and-cream-cheese base is doing real work — a canned-soup version separates into oil and grit on a reheat from frozen.
- Drain the Ro-Tel hard. Extra tomato liquid thins the sauce, and it never fully recovers after a freeze and reheat.
- Cayenne is a dial: half a teaspoon is a warm background, a full teaspoon for a table that wants it — but in the sauce, never sprinkled at the end.

## Rescues

- **Sauce broke into oil and grit** — The cream cheese boiled. Whisk in a splash of cold milk off the heat; if it will not come back, it is still edible under enough cheese, and next time step 4 happens off the burner.
- **Thin and loose** — The Ro-Tel went in wet. Simmer the sauce down before the chicken joins, and drain harder next time.
- **Tortillas vanished into mush** — Whole tortillas laid flat instead of torn quarters, or the pan over-sauced. Quarters make structure; keep the layers even.

## Keeps

- Freezer, unbaked: 3 months. Cool completely — counter, then uncovered in the fridge — then plastic pressed flat to the surface, foil over the top. Label before it goes in.
- From frozen: 175 °C / 350 °F, 70 minutes covered plus 20 uncovered. From thawed: 25 plus 15.
- The sauce alone holds 3 days refrigerated — layering a cold sauce is faster and cleaner than layering a hot one.

## Note

The Texas classic, rebuilt so it survives the freezer intact. Serve
with cold beer, shredded iceberg with lime, and hot sauce on the
table. A rotisserie bird covers the four cups of chicken without
turning on a burner.
