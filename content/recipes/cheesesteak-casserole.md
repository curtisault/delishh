## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Wide skillet, for the steak and vegetables
- Medium saucepan, for the cheese sauce
- Sheet pan at bake time, for the bread

## Ingredients

### Filling — the part that freezes

- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 900 g shaved steak
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 yellow onions, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 225 g mushrooms, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce

### Cheese sauce

- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups whole milk
- 115 g white American cheese, torn
- 115 g provolone, torn

### Bread and top — at bake time, never frozen

- 450 g hoagie rolls, in 3 cm cubes
- 4 tbsp melted butter
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 8 slices provolone

## Steps

1. Heat half the oil over high until it shimmers. Sear the steak in
   two batches with the salt and pepper until browned at the edges,
   then lift it out. Crowded, it boils in its own juice.
   cue: 6 MIN · IN BATCHES, BROWNED EDGES

2. Lower to medium-high, add the rest of the oil, and cook the
   onions, pepper and mushrooms until the mushroom liquid has gone
   and the onions are golden. Add the garlic for 30 seconds, then
   the steak, its juices and the Worcestershire. Cook until the pan
   is dry again.
   cue: 15 MIN · ONIONS GOLDEN, PAN DRY

3. Melt the butter in the saucepan over medium, stir in the flour
   for a minute, then whisk in the milk slowly. Simmer until thick,
   then take it off the heat and stir in the American cheese and
   provolone until smooth.
   cue: 8 MIN · THICK, SMOOTH, OFF THE HEAT

4. Fold the cheese sauce through the steak and vegetables. To
   freeze: spread in the pan, cool completely, and wrap. No bread.
   cue: FREEZE THE FILLING ONLY

5. On baking day, heat the filling covered at 190 °C / 375 °F until
   hot through. Meanwhile, spread the bread cubes
   on the sheet pan, drizzle with the melted butter and garlic
   powder, turn them with your hands, and toast on the rack below
   until crisp at the edges.
   cue: 10 MIN · BREAD CRISP AT THE EDGES

6. Fold the toasted bread into the hot filling, lay the provolone
   slices over the top, and bake uncovered until the cheese is
   melted and browned in spots.
   cue: 25 MIN · CHEESE BROWNED IN SPOTS

## Watchpoints

- The bread never goes into the freezer; frozen bread in sauce bakes up as wet sponge.
- Cook the vegetables until the pan is dry — their water thins the cheese sauce into soup.
- American cheese is what keeps the sauce smooth; provolone alone turns stringy and greasy.

## Rescues

- **Soggy, wet bread** — It sat in the filling too long, or was not toasted. Toast until crisp at the edges and fold it in right before the final bake.
- **Greasy, broken cheese sauce** — It boiled after the cheese went in. Whisk in a splash of cold milk off the heat.
- **Steak grey and chewy** — The pan was crowded. Sear in batches over real heat.

## Keeps

- Freezer, filling only: 3 months. Plastic pressed onto the surface, foil over the top; bag the bread separately and freeze it too if you like.
- From frozen: 190 °C / 375 °F, 50 minutes covered to heat the filling, then the toasted bread and 25 uncovered.
- From thawed overnight in the fridge: 20 minutes covered, then bread and 25 uncovered.
- Baked leftovers: fridge 3 days; the bread softens.

## Note

Shaved steak from the meat case saves you the slicing. If you can only
find a whole ribeye or sirloin, freeze it for 30 minutes and slice it
as thin as you can manage. Cheez Whiz instead of the American cheese
is legitimate and I won't hear otherwise.
