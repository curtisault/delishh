## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Wide skillet or Dutch oven, for the filling
- Mixing bowl and offset spatula at bake time, for the masa — it is never made ahead

## Ingredients

### Filling — the only part that freezes

- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 900 g ground beef
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 425 g can tomato sauce
- 115 g can diced green chiles
- 1 can black beans, 425 g, rinsed
- 1 cup corn kernels
- ½ cup beef broth
- 1 tsp kosher salt

### Masa crust — mixed at bake time, never frozen

- 2 cups masa harina
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ cup lard or neutral oil, melted
- 2 cups chicken broth, warm
- 1 cup Monterey Jack, shredded

## Steps

1. Heat the oil over high and brown the beef in two batches until
   it has a real crust.
   cue: 10 MIN · IN BATCHES, REAL CRUST

2. Add the onion and pepper and cook until soft, then the garlic
   and the dry spices for a minute, then the tomato paste until it
   darkens.
   cue: 8 MIN · SPICES BLOOMED, PASTE DARKENED

3. Add the tomato sauce, green chiles, beans, corn, broth and salt.
   Simmer uncovered until scoopable, not soupy — loose filling
   floods the masa from underneath.
   cue: 15 MIN · SCOOPABLE, NOT SOUPY

4. Spread the filling in the pan, cool completely and wrap. This is
   the whole freezer stage — no crust.
   cue: FREEZE BARE · THE CRUST COMES LATER

5. On baking day, heat the filling covered at 190 °C / 375 °F until
   bubbling all the way to the center.
   cue: 60 MIN FROM FROZEN · 25 FROM THAWED · BUBBLING

6. While it heats, stir the masa harina, baking powder and salt
   together, then the melted fat, then the warm broth. Beat until it
   is a thick, spreadable paste like soft hummus; add broth a
   spoonful at a time if it cracks.
   cue: 3 MIN · SPREADABLE, NO CRACKS

7. Spread the masa over the hot filling in dollops, then join them
   with the spatula to the edges. Scatter the cheese and bake
   uncovered until the masa is set and golden at the rim.
   cue: 35 MIN · SET, GOLDEN RIM

8. Rest before cutting.
   cue: 10 MIN · CRUST FIRMS UP

## Watchpoints

- The masa only ever goes onto bubbling filling; on cold filling the underside stays raw.
- The masa is always mixed fresh. Frozen raw masa bakes dense and sour.
- Simmer the filling to scoopable before it goes in the pan; it loosens again in the oven.

## Rescues

- **Masa raw or gummy underneath** — The filling was not hot when the crust went on. Nothing fixes this pan; next time, bubbling means bubbling.
- **Masa cracked and dry on top** — The paste was too stiff. Beat in more warm broth until it spreads like hummus.
- **Filling bubbled up through the crust** — It went in soupy, or the crust did not reach the edges. Spread to the walls and simmer the filling longer.

## Keeps

- Freezer, filling only: 4 months. Cool completely — counter, then uncovered in the fridge — then plastic pressed flat, foil over the top. Label before it goes in.
- From frozen: 190 °C / 375 °F, 60 minutes covered, then the fresh masa and 35 uncovered.
- Baked leftovers: fridge 4 days.

## Note

This sits next to the chili bake in the archive on purpose, and it is
not the same dish: the lid here is masa, like the outside of a
tamale, and it bakes up soft and corny where cornbread is cakey and
sweet. Lard makes the better crust; oil is fine. Serve with sour
cream, pickled jalapeños and hot sauce.
