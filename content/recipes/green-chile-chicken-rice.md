## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Wide pot with a lid, for the rice

## Ingredients

### Casserole

- 2 cups long-grain white rice — white only; see Watchpoints
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 450 g roasted green chiles, chopped
- 3½ cups chicken broth
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1½ tsp salt
- 4 cups shredded chicken
- 1 cup full-fat sour cream — no substitutes
- 3 cups Monterey Jack, shredded

### To finish

- 1 lime, cut in wedges
- ½ cup cilantro
- 2 scallions, sliced

## Steps

1. Oil over medium. Cook the onion until translucent, then the
   garlic, cumin, and oregano for a minute.
   cue: 6 MIN · TRANSLUCENT, SPICES BLOOMED

2. Stir the rice through until the grains look chalky at the
   edges.
   cue: 2 MIN · CHALKY AT THE EDGES

3. Add the broth, green chiles, and salt. Boil, cover, and cook
   low — about 4 minutes short of done — then rest covered 5
   minutes and fluff. Rice keeps absorbing liquid right up until
   it is served; pull it early.
   cue: 12 MIN · FOUR SHORT OF DONE, REST 5

4. Let the pan sit off the heat before the dairy goes anywhere
   near it.
   cue: OFF THE HEAT · WARM, NOT STEAMING

5. Fold in the sour cream gently, then the chicken and half the
   cheese, and adjust the salt. Sour cream added to a hot pan
   curdles on the spot, and freezing sets that texture
   permanently.
   cue: GENTLY, AND NEVER INTO A HOT PAN

6. Spread in the pan, cover with the remaining cheese, cool
   fully, and wrap for the freezer.
   cue: CHEESE CAP ON, THEN COOL AND WRAP

7. Or bake now at 175 °C / 350 °F until the cheese browns at the
   edges.
   cue: 25 MIN · CHEESE BROWNED AT THE EDGES

## Watchpoints

- Full-fat sour cream only. Light sour cream and yogurt both break into a grainy mess through a freeze and reheat, and no amount of stirring brings them back.
- Long-grain white rice only. Brown and parboiled both go chalky after freezing.
- Mild as written. A minced serrano in with the onion is the cleanest way to add heat without muddying it.

## Rescues

- **Grainy, curdled sauce** — The sour cream met a hot pan, or it was light. There is no bringing it back; cool the pan first next time.
- **Mushy rice** — Cooked to done before assembly. Four minutes short is the recipe.
- **Watery under the cheese** — Canned chiles went in wet. Drain them well.

## Keeps

- Freezer, unbaked: 3 months. Cool completely — counter, then uncovered in the fridge — then plastic pressed flat, foil over the top. Label before it goes in.
- From frozen: 175 °C / 350 °F, 65 minutes covered plus 15 uncovered. From thawed: 25 plus 10.
- Freeze flat and stack once solid.

## Note

Lime squeezed over each portion, then cilantro and scallion — the
acid wakes the whole dish up after a freeze, which is why the finish
never goes in the pan. Frozen roasted Hatch chiles beat canned by a
wide margin if you can find them; canned works, drained well.
