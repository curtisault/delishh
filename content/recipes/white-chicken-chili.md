## Equipment

- Dutch oven or heavy pot
- Blender, for the bean purée
- Quart freezer bags — this one freezes flat in bags, not in a dish

## Ingredients

### Chili

- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 jalapeños, seeded and minced
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 cans great northern beans, 425 g each, rinsed
- 5 cups chicken broth
- 225 g roasted green chiles
- 900 g boneless chicken thighs
- 1½ tsp salt

### To finish — after reheating, never before

- 1 lime, juiced
- ½ cup cilantro
- sour cream, avocado, tortilla chips

## Steps

1. Oil over medium. Cook the onion and jalapeño until soft, then
   the garlic, cumin, coriander, and oregano for a minute.
   cue: 7 MIN · SOFT, SPICES BLOOMED

2. Blend one can of beans with 1 cup of the broth until smooth.
   This is the entire body of the chili — no cream means nothing
   to break in the freezer.
   cue: SMOOTH PURÉE, ONE CAN ONLY

3. Add the remaining broth, the purée, the whole beans, and the
   green chiles.
   cue: EVERYTHING IN, PURÉE INCLUDED

4. Nestle the thighs in whole and simmer partly covered. Whole
   thighs poached in the broth stay moist — pre-cut chicken goes
   stringy.
   cue: 25 MIN · WHOLE THIGHS, BARE SIMMER

5. Pull the chicken, shred with two forks, return it, and salt.
   Simmer a few minutes more.
   cue: 5 MIN · SHREDDED AND BACK IN

6. Judge the body: loose but not watery — it thickens noticeably
   as it cools.
   cue: LOOSE, NOT WATERY

7. Off the heat, add nothing else, and cool fully before it goes
   near the freezer. The lime goes in after reheating — acid
   dulls and turns faintly bitter over weeks in the cold.
   cue: NO LIME YET · COOL COMPLETELY

## Watchpoints

- The purée is the thickener. In a slow cooker, everything except the purée goes in for 4 hours on high; blend and stir the purée in at the end or it settles and scorches.
- Two jalapeños, seeded, is mild. Leave the seeds in one for a real but manageable burn.
- Cool completely before bagging — counter, then uncovered in the fridge until cold.

## Rescues

- **Stringy chicken** — It went in pre-cut, or boiled hard. Whole thighs at a bare simmer, then shred.
- **Thin after reheat** — The bag went in warm and iced up, or the body was watery at the pot. Simmer it down uncovered for a few minutes; it recovers.
- **Faintly bitter out of the freezer** — Lime went in before the freeze. Finish at the bowl, always.

## Keeps

- Freezer, in bags: 4 months. Ladle 4 cups into a quart bag, press the air out, seal, lay flat until solid, then file upright like books — eight cups of chili in two flat bags occupies roughly the footprint of a paperback.
- From frozen: straight to the stove, covered, 20–25 minutes. From thawed: 10 minutes.
- Label before it goes in. Nobody labels it after.

## Note

Creamy from a puréed can of beans rather than dairy, which is
exactly why it freezes clean. Finish every bowl at the table: lime,
cilantro, avocado, crushed tortilla chips, and a spoonful of sour
cream stirred in — the dairy lives in the bowl, never the pot.
