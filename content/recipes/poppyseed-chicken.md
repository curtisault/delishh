## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Medium saucepan, for the sauce
- Zip-top bag, for crushing the crackers and freezing the topping

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 4 tbsp butter
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 1½ cups chicken broth
- ½ cup whole milk
- 1 cup full-fat sour cream — no substitutes
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp poppy seeds
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 4 cups shredded rotisserie chicken

### Cracker crust — bagged apart, always

- 200 g butter crackers, crushed
- 6 tbsp melted butter
- 1 tsp poppy seeds

## Steps

1. Melt the butter over medium, stir in the flour and cook,
   stirring, until it smells toasty.
   cue: 2 MIN · NO RAW FLOUR SMELL

2. Whisk in the broth slowly, then the milk, and simmer until it
   coats a spoon.
   cue: 5 MIN · COATS A SPOON

3. Off the heat, whisk in the sour cream, lemon juice, poppy seeds,
   garlic powder, onion powder, salt and pepper. Fold in the
   chicken and spread it in the pan.
   cue: OFF THE HEAT · EVENLY COATED

4. Crush the crackers in the bag to coarse crumbs — some pieces the
   size of a pea — and mix in the melted butter and poppy seeds.
   cue: COARSE CRUMBS · EVENLY BUTTERED

5. To freeze: cool the filling completely and wrap; freeze the
   crumbs in their sealed bag beside it. To bake now: scatter the
   crumbs over and bake at 175 °C / 350 °F until bubbling at the
   edges and the crust is deep golden.
   cue: 30 MIN · BUBBLING, CRUST DEEP GOLDEN

## Watchpoints

- The crumbs freeze in their own bag and go on at bake time — on frozen filling they turn to paste.
- The sour cream goes in off the heat, or it curdles.
- Full-fat sour cream only; light versions split after freezing.

## Rescues

- **Sauce curdled or grainy** — The sour cream hit a boil. Whisk hard off the heat with a splash of cold milk; it softens but does not fully recover.
- **Soggy topping** — It went on too early. Bake the filling hot first, then crumbs for the uncovered stretch.
- **Topping browning too fast** — Lay foil loosely over for the last 10 minutes.

## Keeps

- Freezer, filling: 3 months, plastic pressed onto the surface, foil over the top. Crumbs in their own sealed bag, taped to the foil.
- From frozen: 175 °C / 350 °F, 50 minutes covered, then crumbs on and 25 uncovered.
- From thawed overnight in the fridge: crumbs on, 30 minutes uncovered.
- Baked leftovers: fridge 4 days.

## Note

The church-potluck version uses a can of cream-of-chicken soup and
a sleeve or two of Ritz. This makes the soup from scratch, which
takes ten minutes and tastes like actual chicken. Serve it over rice,
the way God and every Southern grandmother intended.
