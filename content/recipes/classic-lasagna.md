## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish — or two 20 cm squares, which freeze faster, thaw faster, and one feeds a weeknight without committing the whole batch
- Wide pot, for the sauce
- Sheet pan, for laying out the underboiled noodles

## Ingredients

### Meat sauce

- 450 g ground beef
- 225 g bulk Italian sausage
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 800 g can crushed tomatoes
- 170 g tomato paste
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1½ tsp salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil

### Layers

- 425 g whole-milk ricotta — whole-milk only; see Watchpoints
- 1 large egg
- ½ cup grated parmesan, plus more for the top
- ¼ cup chopped parsley
- 450 g low-moisture mozzarella, shredded
- 12 lasagna noodles, pulled 2 minutes early
- ¼ tsp black pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in the wide pot over medium-high and brown the beef
   and sausage hard, breaking it up. Do not rush this — the browned
   fond is the entire flavor of the sauce.
   cue: 8 MIN · BROWNED HARD, REAL FOND

2. Add the onion and cook 5 minutes. Add the garlic and dried herbs
   for 1 minute, stir in the tomato paste and let it darken 2
   minutes, then add the crushed tomatoes, sugar, and salt. Simmer
   uncovered until it mounds on a spoon.
   cue: 30 MIN · MOUNDS ON A SPOON

3. Boil the noodles 2 minutes short of the box time, drain, and lay
   them flat on the sheet pan. They finish in the oven and stay firm
   through the freeze — fully cooked noodles turn to paste.
   cue: TWO MINUTES SHORT, LAID FLAT

4. Mix the ricotta with the egg, parmesan, parsley, and pepper.
   cue: ONE BOWL, EVEN THROUGHOUT

5. Spread a thin scrape of sauce in the pan, then noodles, ricotta,
   sauce, mozzarella. Repeat twice more — three full layers.
   cue: NOODLES · RICOTTA · SAUCE · CHEESE, THREE TIMES

6. Cap with a final layer of noodles, the last of the sauce, and a
   heavy blanket of mozzarella and parmesan.
   cue: HEAVY BLANKET ON TOP

7. Freeze here — cool fully and wrap. Or bake now at 190 °C /
   375 °F, covered 35 minutes, uncovered 15, and rest 20 before
   cutting.
   cue: 35 MIN COVERED + 15 · REST 20

## Watchpoints

- Whole-milk ricotta only. Skim-milk ricotta weeps in the freezer and leaves a puddle under the top layer.
- Cool completely before wrapping — an hour on the counter, then uncovered in the fridge until properly cold. Warm food steams inside its wrapping, and the ice that forms is what ruins texture.
- Freeze unbaked. The noodles and cheese go through one bake, not two.

## Rescues

- **Watery under the top layer** — Skim ricotta, or the pan was wrapped warm. Blot, bake uncovered a few extra minutes, and fix the ricotta next batch.
- **Noodles turned to paste** — They were fully cooked before assembly. Two minutes short of the box, every time.
- **Center still cold from frozen** — The pan needed the full covered stretch. Keep it covered and give it 15 more; the top browns in the uncovered finish.

## Keeps

- Freezer, unbaked: 3 months. Plastic pressed flat to the surface, foil over the top — air is the enemy, not cold. Label before it goes in; nobody labels it after.
- From frozen: 190 °C / 375 °F, 75 minutes covered plus 20 uncovered. From thawed: 35 plus 15. Rest 20 minutes before cutting, either way.
- The sauce alone keeps 5 days refrigerated or 6 months frozen — assembling from cold sauce is a 20-minute job.

## Note

One of ten freezer casseroles, and the anchor of the set. Serve it
with garlic bread and a sharp, vinegary salad to cut the richness.
Splitting the batch into two square pans is the better move more
often than not — one for tonight, one straight into the freezer.
