## Equipment

- 23 × 33 cm baking dish
- Wide saucepan, for the red sauce
- Dry skillet, for softening the tortillas

## Ingredients

### Red sauce — a toasted flour-and-spice roux, not a can

- 3 tbsp neutral oil
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 3 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 425 g can tomato sauce
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 tsp salt

### Filling

- 900 g chicken thighs, cooked and shredded — about 4 cups
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 115 g can diced green chiles
- 425 g can black beans, rinsed
- 18 corn tortillas — corn, never flour
- 450 g Monterey Jack and sharp cheddar, shredded
- ½ cup cilantro — at serving only

## Steps

1. Heat the oil over medium. Whisk in the flour and cook 1 minute,
   then add the chili powder, cumin, oregano, and garlic powder for
   30 seconds — only until it smells toasty. Past that the chile
   powder turns bitter, and there is no taking it back.
   cue: 2 MIN · TOASTY, NEVER PAST IT

2. Whisk in the tomato sauce and broth. Simmer until it coats a
   spoon, then salt to taste.
   cue: 10 MIN · COATS A SPOON

3. Brush the tortillas lightly with oil and warm them in the dry
   skillet or a 175 °C / 350 °F oven. Cold tortillas crack when you
   layer them and disintegrate on reheat.
   cue: 3 MIN · WARM AND PLIABLE

4. Fold the shredded chicken with the onion, green chiles, black
   beans, and about a cup of the sauce.
   cue: EVERYTHING COATED, NOT SWIMMING

5. Spread sauce in the pan, then 6 overlapping tortillas, half the
   chicken, a third of the cheese, and more sauce. Repeat.
   cue: SAUCE · TORTILLAS · CHICKEN · CHEESE, TWICE

6. Cap with the final 6 tortillas, the remaining sauce, and the rest
   of the cheese. Hold the cilantro — frozen in place it turns black
   and tastes like nothing.
   cue: CILANTRO STAYS OUT OF THE PAN

7. Freeze here — cool fully and wrap. Or bake at 190 °C / 375 °F
   until bubbling at the edges.
   cue: 30 MIN · BUBBLING AT THE EDGES

## Watchpoints

- Corn tortillas hold their structure through a freeze; flour tortillas go gummy. No substituting, even if flour is what is in the drawer.
- Sauce generously. The tortillas drink liquid in the freezer, and a dry pan cannot be rescued once it is on the table — err wet.
- The sauce is mild by design. For real heat, add 2 minced chipotles in adobo to the filling rather than more chili powder.

## Rescues

- **Sauce tastes bitter** — The spices went past toasty in the roux. There is no fix in the pan; start the sauce again — it is twelve minutes.
- **Dry out of the oven** — Under-sauced before the freeze. Serve with warmed extra sauce or crema over the top, and err wet next assembly.
- **Tortillas disintegrated** — They went in cold and unbrushed, or they were flour. Warm, oiled, corn.

## Keeps

- Freezer, unbaked: 3 months. Cool completely — counter, then uncovered in the fridge until cold — then plastic pressed flat to the surface, foil over the top. Label before it goes in.
- From frozen: 190 °C / 375 °F, 60 minutes covered plus 15 uncovered. From thawed: 25 plus 10.
- Freeze flat and stack once solid.

## Note

Two rotisserie chickens, skin discarded, cover the four cups of meat
and save forty minutes. Serve with sour cream, sliced radish,
quick-pickled red onion, and the cilantro added at the table — the
garnish is doing the brightness the freezer took out.
