# delishh — the decisions archive

> Rulings that were made and later overturned, kept as they were
> made, with the date and the ruling that replaced them. Companion to
> `docs/decisions.md`, which holds everything in force. Created
> 2026-09-28, when the decision logs were migrated out of the four
> implementation plans.

**Why keep them.** The reason a thing was tried and reversed is the
reason it is not tried again. A row here is never edited: it is the
ruling as it stood, and the *Superseded by* column is the only thing
added. When a ruling in `decisions.md` is overturned, its row moves
here in the same commit, so the two files never hold two answers to
one question.
